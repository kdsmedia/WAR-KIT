WARKIT — Idle Warung Indonesia
Grow a tiny wooden warung into Indonesia's biggest warung empire!

Welcome to WARKIT, an idle simulation game with an Indonesian warung (small
shop) theme. Start from a simple wooden stall, serve customers, manage stock,
grow your business, and earn the title "Raja Warung Indonesia"!

KEY FEATURES:
• 50+ warung products: groceries, food, drinks, veggies, regional specialties
• Shelf & warehouse: restock on time so shelves never run empty
• Hire & train staff: cashier, stocker, cleaner, manager
• Suppliers & fresh goods: keep veggies/fruits fresh to avoid spoilage
• Weather & time: day/night & rain/clear affect customer flow
• VIP & reputation: serve VIP customers for reputation bonus
• Decorations: decorate your warung to boost comfort & appeal
• Promotions: discounts, buy-2-get-1, bundles, cashback
• Branch system: open branches in cities, manage auto-profit
• Branch managers: hire Pak Hendra to boost branch efficiency
• Distribution vehicles: motorcycle, pickup, box truck, trailer
• Seasonal events: Ramadhan, Lebaran, Shopping Festival
• Investor: prove stable warung for 30 days for capital bonus
• Wholesale store: open a wholesale shop for big daily income
• Tax system: pay business, electricity, water, wage, maintenance tax
• Buildings & facilities: upgrade Wooden → Semi → Modern → Minimarket
• Province expansion: open 10 provinces (West Java, Central Java, etc.)
• Regional products: Cassava Chips, Getuk, Prawn Crackers, Toraja Coffee
• Membership: Silver, Gold, Platinum (loyal customers & bigger bills)
• Digital payments: Cash, Debit, QRIS, Digital Wallet
• National distribution center: manage stock across all branches
• Supply crisis: make quick decisions during distribution disruptions
• National award: earn cutscene & national reputation bonus
• National competition: face big retail competitors
• Raja Warung: earn the highest title + gold skin & mascot statue
• Endless Empire: endless mode with 13 achievements & prestige

HOW TO PLAY:
• Serve customers coming to your warung.
• Restock shelves from warehouse to keep products available.
• Collect money & level up to unlock new features.
• Open branches & provinces to grow a national network.
• Earn all achievements in Endless Empire mode!

Free & offline — play anytime, anywhere.
